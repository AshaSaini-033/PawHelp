# Personal Reflection — PawHelp

> **Write this section yourself, after the visit and implementation.** The assignment specifically asks for a personal reflection written without AI. This file intentionally provides a structure, not a pre-written personal story. Do not submit generic or invented experiences.

Aim for approximately half a page. Use first person and refer to things that actually happened.

## 1. What I got wrong initially
What did I assume about the problem before talking to someone? Which assumption turned out to be incomplete or wrong?

[Write 3–4 honest sentences.]

## 2. What I cut and why
Which feature or idea did I remove to keep the solution small? What did the real person's feedback or the time limit teach me?

[Write 2–3 honest sentences.]

## 3. Where AI helped
Name one concrete task where AI saved time (for example, explaining a test failure, suggesting a CSV validation edge case, or improving documentation). What did you verify yourself?

[Write 2–3 honest sentences.]

## 4. Where AI wasted time or was wrong
Name one suggestion that did not fit the actual problem, was technically incorrect, or needed substantial correction. How did you notice?

[Write 2–3 honest sentences.]

## 5. What I learned from fieldwork
What did the person say or do that you could not have learned by guessing? Mention the real observation or surprise.

[Write 2–3 honest sentences.]

## 6. What I would change next
If I had another day, what is the single most useful improvement, based on the person's actual feedback?

[Write 2–3 honest sentences.]
